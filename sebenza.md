## Features Section

**Section Header:** Everything You Need to Run Your Business
**Subheader:** Sebenza Systems provides a complete suite of integrated tools designed to streamline operations, enhance productivity, and drive growth across every department.

### 12 Core Features:
1. **Customer Relationship Management** - Manage clients, track interactions, generate estimates and invoices, and maintain detailed communication history all in one place.
2. **Inventory & Stock Management** - Track stock across multiple warehouses, manage transfers, monitor stock levels, and generate comprehensive manifests with real-time updates.
3. **HR & Payroll Management** - Streamline employee management, automate payroll processing, track attendance, and maintain comprehensive employee records effortlessly.
4. **Accounting & Finance** - Manage expenses, track payments, generate financial reports, and maintain accurate books with integrated accounting tools.
5. **Document Management** - Upload, organize, and share documents securely. Attach files to clients, projects, or transactions for easy access.
6. **Advanced Analytics** - Get actionable insights with comprehensive reports, visual dashboards, and data-driven recommendations.
7. **Enterprise Security** - Role-based access control, data encryption, secure authentication, and compliance with industry standards.
8. **Task & Project Management** - Create tasks, set deadlines, track progress, schedule meetings, and collaborate with your team in real-time.
9. **Automation & Workflows** - Automate repetitive tasks, set up custom workflows, and integrate with your favorite tools seamlessly.
10. **Multi-Currency Support** - Operate globally with support for multiple currencies, languages, and international payment methods.
11. **Real-Time Reporting** - Access up-to-date reports and dashboards with live data synchronization across all modules.
12. **Mobile Responsive** - Access your business data anywhere, anytime from any device with our fully responsive platform.
